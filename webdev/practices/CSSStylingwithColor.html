Complementary Colors

Complementary colors are when the colors compliment eachother which means that they look good together because they compliment eachother.

Analogous Colors

Analogous colors are colors that like pretty similar, but not the same.

Triadic Colors

Triadic colors are primary, secondary, and tertiary colors. which means that its colorschemes that use 3 colors that are equally spaced out around the color wheel. For example, red, yellow, and blue are primary colors and they're also considered triadic.
