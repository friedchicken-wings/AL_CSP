Zion National Park

Zion national park is a very beautiful place. It has rivers, trees, plants, and especially rocks. Zion is located in Sprindale Utah which is about 95 miles away from the Grand Canyon. If you want to visit the Zion national park, tickets are 35 dollars! You can either get a hotel that's close by, or even camp there! Their camp sites are around 20-30 dollars a night for individual sites and 50-130 dollars a night for group sites.

Why should I specifically go to Zion?

Here I'll give you a list!

  • It has the most greenery out of all the 5 national parks
  • It's a very popular hiking trail
  • It has waterfalls, sandstone cliffs, and rivers
  • They just recently opened a historic bridge connecting Zion Lodge to the popular Emeral Pools Trail system.
  • Zion even has their own hot springs and the massive Black Desert Resort.

When is a good time to go visit Zion?

A good time to go visit Zion is around April-May when it's spring time, and September-October when it's fall. These are the times that the national park is at its most colorful times. During spring, you'll see flowers blooming, trees and plants turning green, and animals wondering around. In the fall, you'll see lots of warm colors such as red, orange, brown, etc. It starts to get colder but it's very beautiful and peaceful since the leaves are falling off to prepare for winter.
