Zion National Park

Zion national park is a very beautiful place. It has rivers, trees, plants, and especially rocks. Zion is located in Sprindale Utah which is about 95 miles away from the Grand Canyon. If you want to visit the Zion national park, tickets are 35 dollars! You can either get a hotel that's close by, or even camp there! Their camp sites are around 20-30 dollars a night for individual sites and 50-130 dollars a night for group sites.
